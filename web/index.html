BEFORE YOUR FIRST BITE

Welcome to the orchard.

You are Super Strawberry. Dig paths, collect cherries, and dodge the green enemies. You start with five lives.

  1. Harvest time · Maps 1 & 4Collect every cherry. Enemies can only move through paths you have already dug.
  2. Falling apples · Maps 2 & 5Collect every cherry and bring all six apples down to the bottom row. Dig underneath them, then get out of the way.
  3. Block puzzle · Maps 3 & 6Push four apple blocks into the four corners. Hold Fire and a direction. Cherry blocks break if there is no room behind them. Shooting is disabled on these maps.

Your shot bounces diagonally through open paths. Catch it again, or wait for it to recharge after a hit. A shot scores 100 points; falling apples normally score 250.

Defeating the entire gang, including reinforcements, also advances you to the next round. Your bonus counts down: at zero, you lose a life. After six maps, the cycle repeats at a faster pace.
